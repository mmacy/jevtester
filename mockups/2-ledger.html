Where it went wrong

Two cases that expected wait came back as parley and fight. The player stood still but spoke, or stood still with a weapon raised. One parley and one evade case came back as wait. The wait option may need a sharper description: "Hold position and watch, without speaking or acting."

What changed from v3 to v4

v4 reworded the parley, evade, and wait descriptions. Six answers changed on decision-1@foundry. Five changes fixed a case and one broke a case. McNemar's test gives p = 0.22, so the gain may be chance.
